# 14 — Hook, retention and conversion playbook

Principle: a hook is a **promise**, and the video must keep it. The first three seconds are a creative constraint for establishing interest. They are not a known algorithmic threshold. All psychological explanations below are INFERRED working theories, to be checked against our own analytics (21).

## Part 2 — Retention through meaningful progression

1. **Beat test.** Every 5–8 s a beat must do at least one of: reveal information · change the situation · raise or resolve stakes · deepen character involvement. Beats that do none are cut, even if they look good.
2. **Ladder of micro-payoffs** (learned from REF01 F03). Answer at least two small questions before the final turn. Open a new one as each closes.
3. **Fork placement.** At about 35–45% of runtime, the viewer is asked to choose before the answer.
4. **Show cause before effect.** Mechanism first (the Sphere), consequence second.
5. **Earned stillness.** One held suspense shot of 2.5–5 s with motion inside it, plus one audio drop.
6. **No arbitrary stimulation.** No cut-every-second rule, no constant zooms, no fake countdowns, no unsupported "99%" claims, no delayed answers that add nothing.
7. **Comprehension guardrails.** Every new place gets an establishing beat ≤1.2 s, and threat positions are consistent (floor plan).
8. **Ending time.** The Verdict holds ≥2.0 s. The final image holds ≥1.0 s before any forward line.

## Part 3 — Conversion: three different viewer decisions

| Decision | Motive | What we build | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Share** | "This could protect someone" (P2), or "you'd argue with this" (P1/P3) | The REAL RULE line as a quotable sentence; a debatable Fork | Engagement bait ("share or…") |
| **Replay** | Missed detail, or wanting to re-check the logic | Planted details (WS-001 non-breathers); rule-consistent clues (WS-024) | Withholding the answer |
| **Subscribe** | A recurring benefit: *a new scenario every day, one decision that matters, rules that stay fair, real rules you can trust* | Consistent cast, Fork/Sphere/Verdict ritual, scenario numbering | Making the ending conditional on following |

**Natural CTAs (only when they fit the story; ≤1.5 s, after the Verdict):**
- Default: **no spoken CTA**. The Verdict ritual and the channel name do the work.
- P2 (share-to-protect), spoken: "Send this to someone who drives at night." (WS-002); "Tell someone before ice season." (WS-021). C1 chip optional.
- P1 debate, pinned comment (not spoken): "Roof or basement — which would you pick?" (WS-007).
- Series bridge, spoken: "December: The Long Winter." (WS-030).
- Subscribe wording, used at most twice a week and only after a strong payoff: C1 chip "New scenario every day". Never "subscribe to see the ending".

**Pinned comment pattern:** one line that adds value (a source, a rewatch prompt, or the debate question), not a demand.
