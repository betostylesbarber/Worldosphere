## 2. One-paragraph summary

A female voice calls "Eli?" from the dark barn. It is Mara, a neighbour covered in someone else's blood. Eli keeps his distance and checks her for bites (none). In a sepia flashback she explains she followed the only sound she could hear — Eli's generator — and "so did they". A map shows ten minutes of engine noise pulling infected toward the farm; Eli kills the generator ("you can't take noise back"). Mara adds that she came in through a break in the west fence by the creek. One infected stands up in the grass, already inside; beyond it, a horde. Final stakes card: "1 KM OF FENCE — 2 PEOPLE · 1 DOG", "too big to defend".

### B. First-frame impact

### C. Script and story structure (MEASURED)
- 208 words in 87.0 s = **143 wpm overall; 171 wpm while speaking**; speech active 84%; **12 pauses >0.4 s** (vs 2 in Part 1) — deliberate dramatic spacing.
- Narration is third-person past tense with character dialogue intercut (Mara).
- Beats: mystery voice → reveal (Mara) → suspicion (blood) → standoff + rule card → bite check → "not my blood" → flashback (how she got here) → twist ("so did they") → consequence map (10 min of noise) → corrective action (kill generator) → "silence… you can't take noise back" → new problem (fence break) → reveal (one inside) → bigger reveal (horde) → stakes card → end card.
- Open questions are answered quickly (barn voice 5.6 s; bites 25.1 s; whose blood 28–30 s) while bigger ones escalate — a ladder of small payoffs (INFERRED as a retention design).
