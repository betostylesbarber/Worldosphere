## 2. One-paragraph summary

A second-person hook ("If the zombie apocalypse starts while you're on a farm, don't run to town") converts into a third-person story about Eli, who has ~30 minutes before the grid fails. The video explains, through infographic shots, why a farm is a strong refuge (land, water, tools, fuel, food), why town is dangerous (everyone converges), why the well stops when the power dies, why a generator is a noise beacon, and why animals make quiet impossible. It ends with a supplies checklist ("on paper, he could survive for weeks") and a reversal: "Someone was already inside the fence."

## 5. Component analysis
