# Worldosphere production recipes (story + edit structures)

Each brief names one recipe. A recipe fixes the *structure*; each episode supplies a fresh story. Percentages are shares of runtime. Word budgets assume an effective 150–165 wpm including pauses. The narrator speaks at 160–175 wpm, with planned 0.4–0.8 s pauses before reveals.

| Runtime | Word budget (narration + dialogue) |
|---|---|
| 50 s | 120–135 words |
| 55 s | 135–150 |
| 60 s | 145–160 |
| 65 s | 160–175 |
| 70 s | 170–185 |

## R-A — Location Outbreak (P1)
| Beat | Share | Must do |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0–3 s | Protagonist + anomaly in frame 0. H1 hook card. First line by 0.3 s |
| Situation | to ~15% | Place, goal, one resource, one threat rule (shown, not lectured) |
| Complication | to ~35% | The place's hidden weakness or a rule consequence. One Sphere if a force matters |
| **Fork** | at ~35–45% | Freeze, split, two labelled options, 1.0–1.6 s total |
| Consequence / escalation | to ~80% | Chosen path plays out. At least one surprise that follows from the rules |
| Turn / payoff | to ~92% | The decisive image (a visual payoff, not narration only) |
| Verdict | last 2–4 s | V1 card over the final image + motif. Optional ≤1.5 s forward line |

Shots: 28–40. Average 1.6–2.0 s. One planned audio drop.

## R-B — Real Rule (P2)
| Beat | Share | Must do |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open in the hazard | 0–3 s | Frame 0 already in danger. The hook states the common instinct or myth |
| Instinct | to ~20% | Show the wrong instinct as a reasonable thing to want |
| **Fork** | ~20–35% | Instinct vs guidance. The unchosen preview shows **why** it fails (physics, verified) |
| Correct action | to ~80% | ≤3 steps, each visualised. Wording comes from the claim ledger |
| Outcome | to ~92% | Survival shown with a consequence still felt (wet, cold, dented car) |
| Verdict + REAL RULE | last 3–4 s | V1 + V3. Sources go in the description |

Never end on the wrong action. No unverified claim survives script lock.

## R-C — Both Endings
| Beat | Share |
|---|---|
| Fork already split on frame 0 + one-line setup | 0–4 s |
| Ending A | ~40% |
| Rewind transition (Fork slides back, 0.6 s, tape-rewind whoosh) | — |
| Ending B | ~40% |
| Side-by-side comparison + Verdict (two statuses) | last ~10% |

Each ending needs its own mini-turn. Both must be plausible under the rules or the facts.

## R-D — Countdown (P2 or P1)
A T1 timer is visible from frame 0. Every beat visibly costs time. The decisive action lands with time left on the clock. The timer must reflect real or story-consistent timing (no fake urgency).

## R-E — World Shift ladder (P3)
Hour 0 cold open → 4–6 "rungs" (HOUR 1, 4, 24, 48, 72) with a clock HUD → one Fork at the rung where a decision matters → checklist payoff → Verdict. State the hypothetical on screen ("In this scenario…"). Every real mechanism must be ledger-verified.

## R-F — Two-part Saga (test only)
- **Part 1** resolves its first question (a standalone payoff) and adds a ≤1.5 s forward line after the Verdict.
- **Part 2** opens with a one-line, in-world recap that a cold viewer understands.
- Link Part 1 to Part 2 with YouTube's related-video feature if it is available for Shorts on your channel (verify in Studio), and with a pinned comment.
- Never make following a condition for the ending.

## R-G — Social deduction
- A closed room and a stated rule (e.g. WR-01) at the top.
- Plant 3 clues: 1 red herring (consistent with the rules) and 2 real clues, with at least one clue visible before the 40% mark.
- The reveal is by observation.
- A humane resolution: no gore, the turning happens off screen.
- Verdict.

## Pacing and editing defaults (all recipes)
- Shot targets: explainer beats 0.8–1.6 s; story beats 1.5–3 s; suspense holds 2.5–5 s with motion inside the shot.
- Every 5–8 s a beat must **reveal, change, escalate or resolve**. If it doesn't, cut it.
- At least two micro-questions are answered before the final turn (the ladder of micro-payoffs).
- Exactly one planned audio drop, paired with a visual hold.
