# 06 — Performance hypotheses and limitations

**Bottom line:**
- **No performance data was available** for the reference account or its videos.
- Its Instagram, YouTube and TikTok pages, and the metric aggregators, could not be reached (egress policy 403 / DNS failures). No search result referenced the account, the series "The Last Farm", or its characters (appendix A).
- So this document **does not** rank the reference videos, infer their retention, completion or follower conversion, or claim a reason for their success.
- What follows are **hypotheses** with their evidence, counterexamples and the test that will check each one on Worldosphere's own channel (21).

- platform distribution changes
- cross-posting.

## 4. Measurements to collect on YouTube
Use the definitions and denominators in 21 §1:
- **viewing choice** (Stayed to watch %)
- engaged views
- average view duration
- average percentage viewed (not a completion rate)
- the retention curve (a spike doesn't prove intentional rewatching)
- shares, subscribers gained, returning vs new viewers
- coded comments.

Derived rates use engaged views as the denominator.

## 5. Limitations that affect every recommendation
1. **Two videos, one story.** Every pattern in 04 is evidence of what this creator did in two episodes, not of what works generally.
2. **No metrics or comments.** Every viewer effect in 04 and 10 is INFERRED.
3. **Research by search snippets only.** No web page was opened. Platform facts (B), tool facts (F) and claim verification (D1, D2) rest on search excerpts and must be confirmed on the live pages (23).
4. **Worldosphere's own channel was not inspected**, so existing uploads, subscribers and audience geography are unknown. The posting time (12:00 ET) is an assumption (17).
5. **No guarantees.** Nothing in this package predicts views, growth or monetization.
