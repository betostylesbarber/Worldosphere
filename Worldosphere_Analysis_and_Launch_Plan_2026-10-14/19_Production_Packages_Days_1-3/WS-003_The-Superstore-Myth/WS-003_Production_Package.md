## 1. Final concept and hook
- **Concept:** "the superstore" is everyone's favourite answer to "where would you go in a zombie outbreak?" Tess, a delivery driver at the store's loading dock at dawn, sees the flaw. Because everyone says it, everyone goes there, and the crowd's noise turns the store into the loudest place in town (WR-03, WR-07). She drives away with a stranded family, and her van turns out to be full of supplies.
- **Selected hook (HP03, "Everyone says…" myth flip):** frame 0 is a high cutaway of the superstore at dawn, with headlights from every road converging on it. Spoken: "Everyone says the superstore is the best place to survive an outbreak. Everyone else says it too." H1: **BEST PLACE TO SURVIVE?**
- **Why it wins:** it names a belief many viewers hold, which creates instant personal relevance and a debate. The image shows the problem (convergence) before it's explained, and the payoff reverses the premise cleanly.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - HP06 impossible-place question: "The outbreak starts while you're at the superstore. Inside or out?" Weaker: no twist.
  - HP05 invisible-threat reveal: frame 0 with the amber Sphere over the store. Strong, but abstract to a cold viewer before the premise is known.

## 16. Fact-check and originality notes
- **Real-world content:** qualitative only (noise carries; crowds are loud). No numbers are presented as fact. "1,200 PEOPLE ARRIVING", "400 CANS" and "60 GAL WATER" are fictional story values on labels. Do **not** add a "how long it lasts" claim unless it is computed from C-1002 (1 gal per person per day).
- **Brand and trademark:** the store is a generic "warehouse superstore" with no real brand name, logo or colour scheme. The van's "UNIT 12" is a fictional fleet number.
- **The radio broadcast** is clearly fictional and generic. No real network names; no emergency-alert tones.
- **Originality versus the reference:** the reference's Part 1 also argues against a crowded destination ("don't run to town"). That is a common survival principle, not a copyable element. WS-003's distinct execution:
  - a different location (the superstore)
  - a different mechanism (crowd noise made visible as a Sphere)
  - a protagonist whose job gives her the edge
  - an ironic, resolved payoff (the van is the store)
  - no farm, generator, dog or cliffhanger.

## 19. Recipe used and why
**R-A Location Outbreak**:
- cold open on a belief (0–6.6 s)
- situation (Tess, cargo, radio)
- complication (the town arrives; noise Sphere)
- **Fork at about 55%**: deliberately later than WS-001's 34%, to test whether a later decision changes retention (see 21, T-08)
- consequence (quiet exit, the town walking toward the noise)
- turn (the van is a store)
- Verdict.

The recipe fits because the episode's value is a twist on a famous debate, resolved by character skill.
