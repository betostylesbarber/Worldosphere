## 1. Final concept and hook
- **Concept:** On Halloween night an outbreak begins on a street where half the people are already dressed as zombies. Kai has three blocks to get his little sister home. Faces and costumes are useless as evidence. Breath is not: on a cold night, the living puff and the Turned never do (WR-02).
- **Selected hook (HP01 Diegetic contradiction):** frame 0 shows an original "MAPLE ROW CITY ALERT" reading *AVOID ANYONE ACTING STRANGELY*, in front of a street full of costumed "zombies". Spoken: "The alert said: avoid anyone acting strangely. It was Halloween." H1 card: **WHO'S IN COSTUME?**
- **Why it wins:** the premise and the question are understood in about 3 s, muted or not. The question is specific ("how do you tell?"), not vague. The answer is fair, physical and checkable, and viewers can re-check it on a rewatch.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - HP06 impossible-place question: "Half the street is dressed as zombies. Some of them aren't dressed." It is weaker because it is verbal only.
  - HP08 rule-first: "If an outbreak starts on Halloween, stop looking at faces. Look at the air." It is weaker because it gives the answer away in the hook.

## 8. Camera directions and animation instructions
- **Camera:** locked off or a slow 2% push. One lateral pan (shot 11) and one track (shot 14). No shake. Push-ins only on realisations (shots 13, 16).
- **Timing:** characters on twos; FX and camera on ones.
- **The breath system is the backbone of the episode.** Build it once as an emitter with three settings: Living (outdoors), Living-indoors (off), Turned (off). Turn on "puff-on-line" for characters who speak outdoors, so a puff blooms at the start of each line.
- **The Fork (09F):**
  - Freeze on the last frame of shot 08.
  - The amber crack draws in over 4 frames.
  - The panels separate by 24 px.
  - F1 chips slide in from the panel edges (4 frames) and hold.
  - At about 1.6 s the left panel scales to full frame (8 frames, ease-in-out), while the right panel desaturates and slides off right (8 frames).
  - Narration continues over the street view.
- **The Sphere (17):** the dome originates at the bucket, rises to a ≈10 m radius in 0.6 s, holds while the rattle sounds, then collapses in 6 frames when Kai clamps the bucket.
- **The Turned:**
  - Shamble cycle 14 frames on twos; lurch 1.4× walk speed.
  - Never blink, never breathe; head ticks of 1 frame.
- **Reaction rule:** 4–6 frames after each stimulus (the scream, Raven's grab, the rattle).
- **Lighting:** amber practical pools on the street. Faces are lit when inside a pool and rim-lit Night Teal outside. Interior: warm key, cold blue window.

## 19. Recipe used and why
**R-A Location Outbreak** (`13_Templates/Production_Recipes.md`):

- situation and stakes (to 15%)
- complication: the real scream (to 25%)
- **Fork at 33%**

- turn and payoff: the window
- Verdict.

The recipe fits because the episode's value is a single, fair, rule-based insight discovered under pressure in a familiar place.
